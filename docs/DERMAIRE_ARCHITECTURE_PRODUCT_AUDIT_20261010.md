## Executive findings

The repository contains substantial real backend functionality and remote Flutter integration. It is a usable engineering foundation, but the official patient experience does not yet express the approved longitudinal Personal Skin Health System. Reuse the authoritative services and tested controllers; implement complete patient flows rather than rebuilding screen collections.

The strongest patient blockers are contradictory image privacy copy, local-only acceptance in the Google onboarding path, disconnected Safety/PSM/clinician projections, and the mismatch between report-only check-ins and Home completion. The guided upload creates a Capture and inline image proxies, separately from the structured CheckIn; Flutter does not show those proxy results. Current production evidence says Vision is disabled, so enabling it is neither necessary nor authorized as a shortcut.

The AI is constrained contextual assistance, not a general agent. It has bounded owner-scoped facts, deterministic safety/clinician guards, validated provider selections, and fail-soft responses. It has no executable tools, durable chat history, complete conversational memory, or proactive event scheduler. The official chat displays only reply/kind, discarding the richer provenance contract.

Doctor backend capabilities exceed Flutter integration. Sharing UI uses a painted placeholder QR and inaccurate access copy; the doctor UI has a nullable-contract defect and messaging is a placeholder. Patient flows take priority; urgent safety and existing clinician instructions must still be surfaced early.

**Still open, not silently resolved:** bottom navigation destinations/count; adherence/gamification; product-card visual design; platform launch order. ## Risk boundaries and retained production contracts

Production/staging/tester URLs must be explicit, immutable per session/build, visibly differentiated outside product medical flows, and tests must deny production hosts by default. A test harness with a production label can still mutate real records; it was never run here. Future staging credentials/data must be synthetic and separate from real testers. Device/debug logs must not include request bodies, tokens, email, report rationale or photo bytes.

Health photos are sensitive. Source strips upload metadata and serves authenticated no-store reads; Flutter must not expose reusable public URLs or store plaintext image caches. Model payload already minimizes identifiers, sensitive disclosures, notes and images; an expanded agent needs an explicit purpose/consent/ownership boundary, not “all app context” by default.

Account deletion persists and verifies independent signed journal intent before external/business deletes, preserving durable privacy across restore. Existing production policy records indefinite journal/key retention, private images, photo soft-delete/version/restore disabled. Do not alter those settings, replace the journal, remove its retained synthetic intents, or use old snapshots as unreviewed restore sources. Photo-only deletion is a future contract, not an existing function. Any new conversation/offline cache/action log must join account/product erasure and restore replay design.

Ops evidence records a frozen independent checkpoint of 17 intents vs 27 primary intents at Phase 5; this is not continuous replication. Startup headroom and uncertified full RTO remain nonblocking product audit debt. No restart/restore/provider drill belongs to this milestone.
